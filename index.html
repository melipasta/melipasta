Ingredients

Egg Whites g

Method

  1. sugar and eggwhites till stiff peaks
  2. mix almond meal and powdered sugar (sieved)
  3. fold sugar into the meringue in several additions until ribbon stage
  4. pipe them out
  5. dry for a few hours
  6. bake at 140 degrees for 10-15 mins
